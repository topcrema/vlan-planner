VLAN Planner — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-23

VLAN Planner does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • The app works fully offline and makes no network connections.
  • VLAN plans you create are stored only on your device and never leave it.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

If you have questions about this policy, open an issue at github.com/topcrema/vlan-planner/issues.
